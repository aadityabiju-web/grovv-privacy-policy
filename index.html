GROVV Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17 September 2026
Last Updated: 17 September 2026

GROVV, operated by O2H2O LLP ("GROVV", "we", "us", or "our"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how GROVV collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with us through our website, WhatsApp Business services, customer-support channels, and related services.

By communicating with GROVV or using our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with GROVV, we may collect the following information:

Information you provide directly

  • Your name
  • Mobile or WhatsApp phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery or service address
  • Information about your plants, gardening requirements, or preferences
  • Product enquiries and purchase information
  • Order and service details
  • Messages, questions, feedback, and other communications you send to us
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting customer support

Information collected through WhatsApp

When you communicate with GROVV through WhatsApp, we may receive information associated with your WhatsApp interaction, including:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your name or WhatsApp profile information made available to us
  • Messages and other content you send to GROVV
  • Conversation timestamps and related communication information
  • Information necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide requested services

Technical information

When you use our online services, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as IP address, browser and device information, operating system, general usage information, and log or diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Provide plant, gardening, landscaping, and related product or service information
  • Recommend relevant products or services based on information you provide
  • Process and manage orders and service requests
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate with you about enquiries, orders, appointments, or services
  • Improve our products, services, and customer experience
  • Maintain conversation and service continuity
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Maintain records required for business, accounting, legal, and regulatory purposes
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations
  • Develop and improve automated customer-support and conversational systems

3. GROVV WhatsApp Assistant and AI

GROVV may use automated technologies, including artificial intelligence ("AI"), to assist with customer conversations.

The GROVV WhatsApp assistant may help with tasks such as:

  • Answering frequently asked questions
  • Providing gardening and plant-care information
  • Recommending relevant products or services
  • Understanding customer enquiries
  • Collecting basic information required to respond to an enquiry
  • Assisting with customer-service and sales conversations

AI-generated responses may not always be completely accurate or suitable for every situation. Customers may request assistance from a human representative where available.

When you communicate with GROVV through WhatsApp, your interaction may also be subject to the applicable terms and privacy practices of WhatsApp and Meta.

GROVV may receive and process information made available through the WhatsApp Business Platform in order to respond to your communications and provide requested products or services.

5. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party technology and service providers to operate GROVV's services. These providers may assist with:

  • Cloud infrastructure and database services

  • Website hosting

Such service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us, subject to appropriate safeguards where applicable.

We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

We may communicate with you regarding enquiries, orders, services, appointments, customer support, and relevant GROVV products, services, offers, or updates where permitted by applicable law and based on the appropriate consent or communication rules.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide requested services, maintain customer-service records, complete transactions, resolve disputes, maintain business records, comply with legal requirements, and protect our services and users.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Customers should avoid sending passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through WhatsApp unless specifically required through an appropriate secure process.
